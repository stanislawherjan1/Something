# Emergent Concept Memory: A Phased Evolution Plan

> Status: design proposal, operator-gated. Scope: `ide-template/` memory subsystem. No new infra, no vector DB, no autonomous writes to canonical surfaces.

## Where we are today

Our durable memory is a **fixed-card model with a lazy-subdir skirt**. The canonical node set is a literal `Set` in code — `CANONICAL_CARDS` in `workspace-api/lib/memory-graph.js:38` holds eight stems (`RULES`, `USER_PROFILE`, `USER_PREFERENCES`, `USER_RELATIONSHIPS`, `USER_REFLECTIONS`, `AGENT_IDENTITY`, `AGENT_TOOLS`, `TEAM`), of which the distill decision tree addresses seven (`reflect-distill.js:42-62` literally says "the 7 cards… walk in order, STOP at the first match… The cards are not a catch-all"; `TEAM` is auto-maintained, not a distill target). The loader (`memory-loader.js:47-65`) concatenates a locked `LOAD_ORDER` behind a ~300-line preamble to clear Anthropic's 4096-token prompt-cache floor; the order is load-bearing and reordering busts the cache. `USER_RELATIONSHIPS` and `USER_REFLECTIONS` are deliberately **not** in `LOAD_ORDER` — read on demand to keep the cached prefix small. That cache discipline is real and worth protecting.

The reflection pipeline is genuinely good and matches the field consensus on episodic→semantic consolidation. `reflect-summary.js` summarises an idle transcript into a confidence-tagged verdict card (`memory/threads/<id>.md`, with `entities`/`decisions`/`open_items`); `reflect-distill.js` reads a 2-day window of durable verdicts (`readRecentDurableVerdicts`, `:88`), proposes least-destructive card edits, and pipes them through `hooks/reflect-apply.py` into `memory/_drafts/` for **operator approval** via `/memory review`. Confidence floors gate writes twice (`CONFIDENCE_FLOORS = {append:0.7, update_field:0.85, replace_section:0.9}`, `reflect-apply.py:60`), every apply logs a before-state sha256 to `.activity.jsonl`, and `resolve_target` (`:242`) hardens the path with `SLUG_RE`/`CARD_RE` + `is_relative_to(MEMORY_DIR)`. Nothing autonomous ever touches a canonical card. Triggers ride one cheap polling process — `bot/recent-snapshot-monitor.sh` curls loopback `/api/internal/reflect-sweep` then `/reflect-distill` every 60s; both are single-flight and self-rate-limited (distill ~12h via `memory/_drafts/.last-distill`).

**The honest gap.** Three things are structurally missing, and a fourth is a coexistence wart. (1) There is **no concept-keyed accretion surface**: a person or project named in 30 verdicts produces 30 source-keyed cards and zero growing note — every durable fact must squeeze into one of seven role-buckets or be dropped. (2) The `entities:` slugs that verdict cards already emit produce wiki edges in `memory-graph.js:254-262`, but `addEdge` returns early unless the target node already exists (`if (!byId.has(targetId)) return`) — **so those cross-thread edges dangle against nothing**. (3) The ~60-line "promote a card to a topic page" rule (`routing-rules.md:39-56`) is prose **no code measures**; cards silently grow past it. (4) A second, fully disjoint store exists — the stock `mcp__memory` knowledge graph (`kg.jsonl`/`memory.jsonl`, per-user-scoped in `claude.js:171-202`) — which `buildMemoryGraph` never reads and the loader never surfaces; the "graph holds structure, cards hold narrative, don't duplicate" contract (`global-claude.md:120`) is currently unenforceable because neither side can see the other.

## Target model

We keep the fixed cards as **frozen summary/role surfaces** and grow depth + accretion into a new **concept layer** that the graph already knows how to link. Mapped to our concepts:

- **Atomic, concept-oriented cards that accrete (Principle A).** A recurring `entities:` slug gets a first-class page `memory/concepts/<slug>.md` (private mirror `memory/users/<slug>/concepts/`). Its body is a `## Claims` list — one atomic, claim-shaped assertion per line, each with inline provenance. A concept mentioned across 30 threads becomes **one growing, citable page**, not 30 source cards. The seven cards keep only a one-line pointer (`→ acme: concepts/acme.md`) so they stay tight and the cached prefix stays untouched.
- **Dense bidirectional links over hierarchy (Principle D/A).** Concept pages become real edge **targets**: the already-emitted verdict `entities:` edges (`memory-graph.js:254-262`) stop dangling and resolve to a concept node; the force-graph finally shows thread→concept accretion.
- **Emergent promotion at a computable squeeze point (Principle B/C).** A concept is born **at a felt friction we can compute**, not on a clock and not at a prose line-count: when a slug recurs across **≥ N distinct verdict threads** (heat, default 3) — i.e. it is a high-in-degree node `buildMemoryGraph` already computes. Below the threshold it stays a mere slug; at/above it, distill proposes the page. MOC graduation is deferred (see Phase 4 / open decisions) — a concept page that itself becomes a high-out-degree hub cleaves into children, but only if it earns its keep.
- **Episodic → semantic reflection, written back (Principle E).** Concept synthesis is a **second output bucket of the existing distill pass** (one Haiku call, not a second cron) so the same machinery that consolidates the 7 cards also accretes concept pages — without doubling cost or risking double-propose.
- **Invalidate-don't-delete + provenance (Principle E).** Concept claims reuse the established `~~…~~ (retired DATE)` strike convention extended with `[Source: thread N, date]`; nothing is mutated in place, every write logs a before-sha256 to `.activity.jsonl`, and the graph thins an entity's edge when claims are struck so temporal state is legible with no schema/DB.
- **LLM-owned health, surfaced not silent (Principle D).** A periodic LINT pass over the small wiki proposes — via a **non-destructive `flag` action** — contradictions, stale claims, orphan pages, missing cross-refs, and INDEX drift, into a `## Lint findings` section. It never mutates a fact and never bypasses `/memory review`.

What we explicitly do **not** adopt: embedding/cosine entity-resolution (no vector infra — we lean on big context), MemGPT-style agent self-edit of canonical memory, and any auto-regenerated page the bot treats as substrate without operator approval. We also do **not** unify the `mcp__memory` KG in this plan (see Risks); the concept layer lives entirely in the markdown tree the graph builder already walks.

## New mechanisms

**1. Squeeze-point concept emergence (heat-triggered).**
*Trigger:* a slug appears in ≥ `REFLECT_CONCEPT_HEAT` (default 3) distinct verdict threads' `entities:` lists — a high-in-degree node `buildMemoryGraph` already computes.
*Procedure:* on the existing distill tick, `computeConceptHeat(actorSlug)` (zero new scanning — reuses `parseEntitiesFromFrontmatter`) filters which entities are eligible. The **existing** distill Haiku call (same spawn, same digest, same `claude -p` Haiku) is asked for a second output bucket: one atomic claim-line per eligible entity, citing its thread. Proposals flow through the unchanged `ingestProposals → reflect-apply.py → _drafts → /memory review` path. No new cron, no new endpoint, no second LLM call.

**2. Concept accretion with per-line provenance.**
*Trigger:* an approved `concept_append` proposal.
*Procedure:* `apply_action` (`reflect-apply.py:371`) creates the page on first write (frontmatter + `## Claims`) and appends `- <claim>  [Source: thread N, YYYY-MM-DD]`. Same atomic tmp-write + before-sha256 `.activity.jsonl` log as canonical cards. Confidence floor `append=0.7` applies, so low-confidence claims never reach the draft.

**3. Dedup-hint (lightweight create-vs-merge, no vectors).**
*Trigger:* every concept proposal for an entity whose page already exists.
*Procedure:* `renderDigest` inlines that page's current `## Claims` body; the SYSTEM line instructs Haiku to NOOP a restated/rephrased claim. Pure prompt-level merge over existing context. **Known limit:** this only suppresses restating an *existing canonical slug*; it does not resolve a concept arriving under a *new* slug (`acme` vs `acme-pricing`). That is left to operator eyes on the dashboard and the LINT "duplicate concept" finding — not solved, deliberately.

**4. LINT health-check (Phase 3, non-destructive).**
*Trigger:* loopback `/internal/memory-lint`, self-rate-limited ~24h (heaviest call), fired by the monitor tick.
*Procedure:* read the whole small wiki in one shot (size-guard shards if it has ballooned), ask Haiku for contradictions / stale claims / orphans / missing-concepts / INDEX-drift, emit `flag` proposals into a `## Lint findings` section via the existing ingest path. **Defaults to `flag` — never mutates a fact.** Operator reviews via the same `/memory review` command.

## Phased rollout

Each phase is independently shippable, end-to-end-tested on a branch, validated on the canary test server before any prod cascade (per the test-server rule), and operator-gated. SKILL/doc-only changes deploy without Docker; `lib/`/`routes/`/monitor changes need a deploy.

**Phase 0 — Cheapest, ship-first hygiene (no new layer).**
- `routing-rules.md`: kill the dead ~60-line rule; document heat as the emergence signal (doc-only).
- `memory-graph.js`: make the dangling `entities:` edges honest **today** — render a synthetic/placeholder concept node for a high-heat slug even before a page exists, so the dashboard stops lying about cross-thread links. (Read-only graph change.)
- Add the hard, written invariant in `memory-loader.js` comment + `memory-cards/SKILL.md`: *concepts/ must NEVER enter `LOAD_ORDER`*.
*Manual:* nothing changes about writes. *Automatic:* graph honesty.

**Phase 1 — Concept layer, read + write, fully operator-gated (the core win).**
- `memory-graph.js`: `concept` fileKind + `computeConceptHeat`.
- `reflect-distill.js`: second output bucket (concept proposals), gated on heat, with the dedup-hint.
- `reflect-apply.py`: `concept_append` action + `kind:"concept"` resolve branch (slug-only, no `CARD_RE` widening).
- `MemoryDashboard.jsx`: render concept nodes + degree-as-radius.
- `memory-cards/SKILL.md`, `INDEX.md` template: document the layer.
*Manual:* every concept page birth is operator-approved via `/memory review`. *Automatic:* heat detection, proposal generation, dashboard visibility. **End-to-end test:** seed ≥3 verdicts naming the same entity → one tick → a concept proposal lands in `_drafts` with correct scope/owner; approve → page created with `## Claims` + provenance; private-entity concept lands under `memory/users/<owner>/concepts/` and its title never reaches shared INDEX (leak parity with topics).

**Phase 2 — Card↔concept pointer discipline + distiller card-state context.**
- Once a concept page exists, distill proposes the originating card carry a pointer line, not the full fact (extend `routing-rules.md:21`).
- Feed the relevant target-card section text into the distiller prompt as `EXISTING in <card>/<section>: …` so it can prefer retire-old-add-new over a duplicate bullet — the single highest-value, lowest-risk graft, needs no new module.
*Manual:* operator still approves. *Automatic:* the distiller stops blind-appending near-duplicates.

**Phase 3 — LINT health-check.**
- New `memory-lint.js` + `/internal/memory-lint` route + one monitor curl + `flag` action in `reflect-apply.py`.
- First finding to wire: INDEX drift, closing the promotion-skipped loop.
*Manual:* operator reviews findings. *Automatic:* periodic contradiction/stale/orphan/missing-concept detection, surfaced never silent.

**Phase 4 — (Deferred / gated on a go-decision) MOC graduation.**
- When a concept page is itself high-out-degree, distill asks the squeeze-point question ("is this secretly several concepts?") and proposes children + a parent rewritten to mostly-pointers. This is the weakest-grounded mechanism and may churn the review queue; ship only after Phases 1–3 prove the heat signal is reliable. Do **not** let it ride the core win's coattails.

## Risks & non-goals

- **Slug fragmentation is the unsolved core problem.** `acme`/`acme-pricing`/`Acme Corp` remain distinct stems under the unchanged exact-stem match (`memory-graph.js:224`); concept pages *amplify* the cost (three near-duplicate first-class pages instead of one messy section). Mitigation is best-effort only: dedup-hint (same canonical slug), heat-gating (reduces churn), LINT "duplicate concept" finding, and operator eyes on the dashboard. **Non-goal: embedding/cosine entity-resolution** — no vector DB; we lean on big context, accept residual duplicates, and let the operator merge.
- **Cache-floor regression.** If concept pages ever entered `LOAD_ORDER` they'd bust the 4096-token cached prefix every turn. **Hard invariant, written into the loader comment:** concepts/ is read-on-demand only, like RELATIONSHIPS/REFLECTIONS.
- **Team-mode privacy.** A private-entity concept must route to `memory/users/<owner>/concepts/` or a private fact leaks into the shared tree readable in every teammate's cached INDEX. Reuse the `resolve_target` scope/owner + `is_relative_to` guard; carry the verdict's owner onto every concept proposal; **SKIP any concept that cannot be attributed — never guess scope.** This must have an explicit leak-parity test.
- **No client-name leaks.** Concept slugs and claim lines are durable text in a public-OSS-adjacent tree. The router rules and the operator-approval gate already scrub; the LINT pass must never echo a real client name into a findings bullet. (Hard rule from prior incidents.)
- **Approval-queue overload.** A chatty week could flood `/memory review`. Mitigation: reuse the under-propose bias + confidence floors + the `MAX_VERDICTS=20` window cap; cap concept proposals per cycle (top-K by heat).
- **Double-propose / double-home.** Folding concepts into the existing distill call (one Haiku call, two buckets) avoids the doubled cost and collision a separate cron would create — but the same fact can still land on both a card and a concept page before the operator notices. Mitigation is the pointer rule (Phase 2) + dedup-hint; **no automated cross-surface contradiction check ships in this plan** beyond LINT's advisory flag.
- **`flag`/`concept` touch the hardened `reflect-apply.py`.** Net-new attack surface on path logic. Keep concept resolution **slug-only** (do not widen `CARD_RE` to permit `concepts/`); test `is_relative_to` against `../` and absolute-path injection before merge.
- **Non-goals, explicit:** no autonomous write to any canonical or concept surface; no auto-regenerated page the bot treats as substrate without approval (this is why we do NOT adopt the runner-up's unapproved community-rollup pages); no MemGPT-style self-edit; and **no unification of the `mcp__memory` KG in this plan** — it stays a separate store, flagged for a future decision rather than bolted on here.

## Open decisions

1. **Heat threshold N.** Default `REFLECT_CONCEPT_HEAT=3` distinct verdict threads. Lower = more concept pages sooner (richer graph, noisier review queue); higher = only the truly recurring earn a page. Pick the starting value and whether it's per-actor tunable.
2. **One Haiku call or two.** Recommended: fold concept synthesis into the **existing** distill pass (one call, two output buckets) to avoid doubled cost and double-propose. Confirm acceptance of the slightly larger distill prompt (it now inlines each eligible concept's `## Claims` body) versus a cleaner-but-pricier second pass.
3. **MOC graduation: ship or shelve (Phase 4).** It's the weakest-grounded mechanism and a likely review-queue churner. Default recommendation: shelve until Phases 1–3 prove out. Scope it now, or explicitly park it?
4. **The two-store question.** The `mcp__memory` KG (`kg.jsonl`) already does name-keyed accretion the bot writes but never reads back. This plan deliberately ignores it to avoid a *third* store. Decision: (a) leave it untouched, (b) deprecate KG writes in favour of concept pages, or (c) later wire concept-page synthesis to also read KG observations. Recommendation: (a) for now, revisit after Phase 1.
5. **Pointer-vs-duplicate enforcement (Phase 2).** When a concept page exists, do cards keep *only* a pointer (tightest prefix, but the fast-path loses the inline fact), or a one-line summary *plus* a pointer (slightly more cached bytes, more self-contained)? This trades cached-prefix size against fast-path completeness.
